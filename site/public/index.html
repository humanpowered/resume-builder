Plumbline

Every result you have earned, written down once and kept true.

Plumbline interviews you about your working life, job by job, and builds a complete record of what you did and what it changed. Then it searches for jobs that fit, and for each one worth applying to, draws the right lines from that record into a two-page resume and cover letter, in your own words.

Testing privately with invited users. Opening early 2027.

How it works

  1. Start from what you have

    Upload an old resume or start blank. Plumbline asks short questions about each job: the challenge you walked into, what you changed, and how you know it worked.

  2. Keep one master record

    Everything lands in a single document that can run far past two pages. You add to it as your career moves, so nothing gets forgotten between job searches.

  3. Find the jobs worth your time

    Each night Plumbline searches job boards and company career pages for postings that match your targets, then scores each one against your record. You see the ones worth applying to, ranked, and skip the rest.

  4. Apply with a tailored resume and cover letter

    For each match, Plumbline draws the experience and skills that fit into a two-page, ATS-readable resume and a cover letter. It keeps every figure you gave it and invents none.
