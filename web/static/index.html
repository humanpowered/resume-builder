Do you have a resume?

Upload a resume or a LinkedIn profile saved as PDF and the interview works from it, asking about what it leaves out. Nothing is rewritten: every line is copied into your record and checked against your file.

Using LinkedIn? How to save your profile as a PDF
  1. Open your own profile on linkedin.com, on a computer.
  2. Under your name and headline, click Resources (on some accounts it is More).
  3. Choose Save to PDF. LinkedIn downloads a file, usually named Profile.pdf.
  4. Choose that file here.

A profile link can't be used: LinkedIn shows profiles only to signed-in members and doesn't let other apps read them.
