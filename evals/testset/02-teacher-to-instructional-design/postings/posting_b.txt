Curriculum Designer, High School Science
Lumenfold Learning - Oakland, CA (Hybrid, 2 days in office)

About Lumenfold
Lumenfold Learning builds digital, phenomenon-based science curriculum used by more than 1,200 middle and high schools. Our materials combine interactive simulations, hands-on labs, and embedded formative assessment, and are designed by former teachers for teachers.

About the Role
The Curriculum Designer will write and revise high school chemistry and physical science lessons, assessments, and teacher materials for our next-generation course. You will work with learning scientists, product managers, and multimedia producers to ensure lessons are rigorous, accessible, and aligned to NGSS.

Responsibilities
- Write student-facing lessons and teacher guides for HS Chemistry and Physical Science units
- Design formative and summative assessments aligned to NGSS performance expectations and three-dimensional learning
- Apply Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles and supports for multilingual learners
- Draft specifications for interactive simulations and videos with the multimedia team
- Incorporate classroom pilot feedback and data into revisions
- Facilitate teacher professional learning webinars for customer districts

Required Qualifications
- 3+ years of classroom teaching experience in secondary science (chemistry strongly preferred)
- Deep understanding of NGSS and three-dimensional science instruction
- Experience writing curriculum, assessments, or professional development materials
- Excellent writing and editing skills
- Experience using an LMS (Canvas, Schoology, or Google Classroom)

Preferred Qualifications
- Master's degree in education or a science field
- Experience in an ed-tech or publishing environment
- Experience with OpenSciEd or other storyline-based curricula
- Familiarity with WCAG accessibility standards
