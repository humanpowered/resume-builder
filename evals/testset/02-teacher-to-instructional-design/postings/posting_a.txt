Instructional Designer II - Clinical Operations Learning
Tamarind Health Partners - Remote (US)

Who We Are
Tamarind Health Partners provides revenue cycle and patient access services to more than 300 hospitals nationwide. Our Learning & Development team builds onboarding and compliance training for 4,500 employees in call centers, coding, and billing operations.

The Role
We are seeking an Instructional Designer to design and develop engaging, performance-focused eLearning and instructor-led training. You will partner with subject matter experts and operations leaders to analyze performance gaps and build solutions that measurably improve productivity and quality.

What You'll Do
- Apply ADDIE and/or SAM to design learning solutions from needs analysis through evaluation
- Build interactive eLearning modules in Articulate Storyline 360 and Rise 360
- Write storyboards, scripts, and assessments aligned to measurable learning objectives
- Partner with SMEs to translate complex processes into job aids, simulations, and microlearning
- Publish SCORM packages to our LMS (Cornerstone) and troubleshoot tracking issues
- Evaluate training effectiveness using Kirkpatrick Levels 1-3 and report results to stakeholders
- Manage multiple projects simultaneously against deadlines

Required Qualifications
- 3+ years of instructional design experience in a corporate or healthcare setting
- Proficiency with Articulate Storyline 360 and Rise 360
- Experience with SCORM and LMS administration
- Strong knowledge of adult learning theory (andragogy) and instructional design models
- Portfolio demonstrating eLearning development work

Preferred Qualifications
- Experience in healthcare revenue cycle or call center training
- Camtasia or Vyond video development
- ATD CPTD certification
- Bachelor's degree in Instructional Design, Education, or related field
