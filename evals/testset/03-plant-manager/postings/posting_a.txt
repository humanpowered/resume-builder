Plant Manager
Northmeadow Foods - Kalamazoo, MI

Company Overview
Northmeadow Foods is a family-owned manufacturer of refrigerated dips, dressings, and sauces sold under our own brands and as private label for major grocery retailers. Our Kalamazoo facility runs 9 filling and packaging lines and employs 260 team members.

Position Summary
The Plant Manager has full accountability for safety, food safety, quality, service, and cost performance of the Kalamazoo facility. Reporting to the VP of Operations, this leader will build a strong safety and food safety culture, develop the leadership team, and drive continuous improvement to support 15% volume growth over the next two years.

Key Responsibilities
- Own plant P&L, operating budget, and capital plan
- Lead a safety culture with goal of zero injuries; ensure OSHA compliance
- Maintain SQF Level 2 certification and FSMA compliance; serve as executive sponsor for HACCP and preventive controls programs
- Drive OEE, yield, and labor productivity improvements using Lean tools
- Lead and develop managers in production, maintenance, quality, sanitation, and warehouse
- Partner with supply chain on production planning, inventory, and customer service levels
- Manage capital projects including a new high-speed filling line in 2027

Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Food Science, or related field
- 8+ years of manufacturing leadership, including 3+ years as plant manager or operations manager
- Experience in food or beverage manufacturing in an FDA- and/or USDA-regulated environment
- Working knowledge of SQF or BRC, HACCP, and GMPs
- Demonstrated Lean manufacturing results (OEE, waste, changeover)

Preferred Qualifications
- PCQI (Preventive Controls Qualified Individual) certification
- Six Sigma Green Belt or Black Belt
- Experience with high-speed filling and packaging equipment
- Non-union and union environment experience
- SAP experience
