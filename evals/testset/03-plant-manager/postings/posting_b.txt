Director of Operations - Molding & Assembly
Corvane Polymer Group - Grand Rapids, MI (on site, travel 40%)

About Corvane
Corvane Polymer Group is a privately held supplier of custom injection-molded and assembled components for the automotive, medical, and appliance markets. We operate four plants in Michigan, Indiana, and Ohio with combined revenue of $410M.

Position Overview
The Director of Operations will lead three of our four plants, reporting to the Chief Operating Officer. This role sets operational strategy, standardizes best practices across sites, and develops plant leadership to deliver safety, quality, delivery, and cost targets.

Responsibilities
- Lead three Plant Managers and their teams; set annual objectives and hold sites accountable to KPIs
- Deploy a common operating system: tiered meetings, standard work, TPM, and problem solving across all plants
- Own combined operating budgets of approximately $150M and drive annual productivity savings of 3-5%
- Ensure IATF 16949 and ISO 13485 certifications are maintained
- Lead new program launches with engineering and program management (APQP/PPAP)
- Develop succession plans and leadership capability across sites
- Support evaluation of capacity, footprint, and automation investments

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Engineering or related technical field
- 12+ years of manufacturing experience with at least 5 years as a Plant Manager
- Multi-site leadership experience
- Injection molding expertise required
- Proven success with IATF 16949 quality systems and automotive customers
- Strong financial acumen, including P&L and capital planning

Preferred
- MBA
- Six Sigma Black Belt
- ISO 13485 medical device experience
- Experience with Plex or other cloud ERP/MES
- Experience leading in union environments
