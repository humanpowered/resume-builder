District Manager - Oklahoma / Arkansas
Sparrowgate Home & Hardware - Tulsa, OK (field-based, 50% travel)

About Sparrowgate
Sparrowgate Home & Hardware operates 240 neighborhood home improvement stores across the South Central US. We combine the selection of a big box with the service of a local hardware store.

Position Summary
The District Manager leads 10-12 stores generating approximately $90M in annual sales. You will coach and develop Store Managers, drive sales and profitability, and ensure consistent execution of company standards, safety, and customer service across the district.

Responsibilities
- Drive district sales, margin, payroll, and shrink performance against plan
- Recruit, hire, develop, and retain Store Managers; build bench strength for future openings
- Conduct regular store visits and operational audits; build action plans with Store Managers
- Analyze P&L and KPI reports to identify opportunities and share best practices across stores
- Partner with Loss Prevention, HR, and Merchandising on district initiatives
- Support new store openings and remodels
- Ensure compliance with safety, labor law, and company policy

Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in business or related field, or equivalent experience
- 3+ years of multi-unit retail management experience
- Proven track record of delivering sales and profit results in a high-volume retail environment
- Experience with P&L analysis, payroll management, and shrink control
- Demonstrated success developing store leaders
- Valid driver's license and ability to travel overnight

Preferred
- Home improvement or hardware retail experience
- New store opening experience
- Bilingual English/Spanish
