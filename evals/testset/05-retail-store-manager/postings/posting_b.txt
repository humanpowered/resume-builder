Store Director
Prairiefield Market - Broken Arrow, OK

Who We Are
Prairiefield Market is an employee-owned regional grocery chain with 58 stores in Oklahoma, Kansas, and Missouri. Our stores feature full-service bakery, deli, meat and seafood, pharmacy, and online grocery pickup.

The Opportunity
The Store Director leads a $32M, 160-associate store and is accountable for the full guest experience, sales, profitability, food safety, and team development.

What You'll Do
- Lead department managers in grocery, produce, meat/seafood, deli/bakery, front end, and online pickup
- Own the store P&L including sales, gross margin, labor, and shrink (including perishable shrink)
- Ensure compliance with food safety, sanitation, and health department requirements
- Drive online grocery pickup performance (order accuracy, on-time pickup)
- Build a culture of guest service and associate engagement
- Recruit, train, and develop department managers and future store leaders
- Maintain inventory control and ordering systems

Requirements
- 5+ years of retail store management experience, including 2+ years as a store manager or assistant store manager
- Grocery or perishables management experience
- ServSafe Food Protection Manager certification (or obtain within 60 days)
- Strong understanding of store P&L, labor scheduling, and inventory management
- Experience leading teams of 100+ associates

Preferred
- Experience with online order pickup / BOPIS operations
- Pharmacy operations experience
- Associate's or Bachelor's degree
