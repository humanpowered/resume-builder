Charge Nurse - Medical/Surgical ICU (Full-Time, Days)
Brightwater University Health - Dayton, OH

About Us
Brightwater University Health is a 620-bed academic medical center and Level I trauma center serving southwest Ohio. We are a Magnet-designated organization with a shared governance model and a commitment to evidence-based practice.

Position Summary
The ICU Charge Nurse coordinates patient care and unit operations for a 28-bed combined Medical/Surgical ICU during the assigned shift. The charge nurse makes patient assignments based on acuity and competency, manages patient throughput with the house supervisor and bed management, serves as a clinical resource to staff, and responds to rapid responses and codes.

Responsibilities
- Make patient assignments based on acuity, skill mix, and continuity of care; adjust staffing in real time
- Coordinate admissions, transfers, and discharges with bed management to support hospital throughput
- Serve as clinical resource for ventilator management, vasoactive titration, CRRT, and post-operative critical care
- Lead the unit's response to rapid response and code events
- Round on patients and staff; escalate safety concerns using chain of command
- Monitor compliance with quality bundles (CLABSI, CAUTI, VAP, pressure injury prevention)
- Precept and mentor new graduate nurses in the Nurse Residency Program
- Participate in unit shared governance and quality improvement initiatives

Required Qualifications
- Current, unrestricted Registered Nurse license in the State of Ohio
- Minimum 3 years of adult ICU experience
- Charge nurse experience
- BLS and ACLS
- CCRN certification, or obtained within 12 months of hire

Preferred Qualifications
- BSN (required for hire after 2027)
- ECMO experience
- Experience precepting new graduate nurses
- CRRT competency
- Experience with Epic
- TNCC
