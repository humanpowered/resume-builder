Software Engineer, Full Stack (Payments)
Fernbrook Financial - Denver, CO (Hybrid, 3 days in office)

Who We Are
Fernbrook Financial builds accounts payable automation for mid-market companies. More than 6,000 businesses use Fernbrook to approve invoices, pay vendors, and reconcile payments with their accounting systems.

The Role
Join our Payments team to build the product experiences and APIs that move more than $2B in vendor payments each month. You'll ship features across a React/TypeScript front end and Python services, working closely with product and design.

Responsibilities
- Build user-facing features in React and TypeScript, from design handoff to production
- Develop and maintain Python (Django) services and REST APIs for payment workflows
- Integrate with banking partners and payment rails (ACH, virtual cards, wire)
- Write well-tested code and participate in code reviews
- Help keep our systems secure and compliant (PCI DSS, SOC 2)
- Contribute to on-call rotation for payments services

Qualifications
- 3+ years of professional experience as a software engineer
- Strong experience with React and TypeScript in production applications
- Experience with Python and a web framework (Django, Flask, or FastAPI)
- Solid understanding of relational databases (PostgreSQL preferred)
- Experience integrating third-party APIs

Preferred
- Experience in fintech, payments, or other regulated industries
- Familiarity with PCI DSS compliance
- Experience with Celery or other task queues
- Experience with idempotency and reliability patterns for financial transactions
